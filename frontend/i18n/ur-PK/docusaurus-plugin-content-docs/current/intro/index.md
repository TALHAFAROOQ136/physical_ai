# Physical AI aur Humanoid Robotics Men Khush Amdeed

Zahin robots banane ke is jamiya kurs men khush amdeed! Ye nasabi kitab aap ko jadid humanoid robotics aur physical AI systems men istemal hone wali zaroori technoloji aur taktikon ki rahnumai kare gi.

## Aap Kya Seekhenge

Is kurs ke ikhtetam tak, aap ye karne ke qabil honge:

- **Robotic control systems banayein** ROS 2 ka istemal karte hue, jo robotics ke liye industry-standard middleware hai
- **Haqiqat pasandana simulations banayein** Gazebo aur NVIDIA Isaac Sim ka istemal karte hue mحفوظ testing ke liye
- **AI perception systems implement karein** vision, sensing, aur mahol ki samjh ke liye
- **Language models ko integrate karein** awaz aur text ke zariye qudrat natural human-robot interaction ke liye

## Kurs Ka Dhancha

Ye kurs char ahem modules aur ek capstone project men taqseem hai:

| Module | Mawzu | Muddat |
|--------|-------|----------|
| 1 | ROS 2 - Robotic a'asir system | 2 hafte |
| 2 | Simulation - Digital jurooa | 2 hafte |
| 3 | NVIDIA Isaac - AI-Robot damagh | 2 hafte |
| 4 | VLA - Vision-Language-Action | 2 hafte |
| Capstone | Apna Humanoid Banayein | 4 hafte |

## Ye Kis Ke Liye Hai?

Ye kurs in ke liye design kiya gaya hai:

- **Software engineers** jo robotics men inteqal karna chahte hain
- **Robotics ke shauqeen** jo zahin nizam banana chahte hain
- **AI practitioners** jo embodied AI aur physical systems men dalechspi rakhte hain
- **Tulaba** jo computer science, electrical engineering, ya mechatronics parh rahe hain

## Shara'it

Shuru k karne se pehle, aap ke pas hona chahiye:

- Buniyadi Python programming ki maharat
- Linux command line se waqfiyat
- Buniyadi riayazi ki samjh (linear algebra, calculus)
- Ubuntu 22.04 (tajveez karda) ya WSL2 ke sath Windows wala computer

Physical AI men apna safar shuru karne ke liye taiyar hain? Aie [shara'it ki janach](/docs/intro/prerequisites) se shuru karte hain!
